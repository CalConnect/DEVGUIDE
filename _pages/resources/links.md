Useful external resources related to calendaring and scheduling.

## Time & Calendars

- [Your Calendrical Fallacy Is...](http://yourcalendricalfallacyis.com/) — Common misconceptions about calendar systems, by Dave Delong
- [Falsehoods Programmers Believe About Time](http://infiniteundo.com/post/25326999628/falsehoods-programmers-believe-about-time) and [More Falsehoods](http://infiniteundo.com/post/25509354022/more-falsehoods-programmers-believe-about-time) — by Noah Sussman
- [UTC is Enough for Everyone ...Right?](https://zachholman.com/talk/utc-is-enough-for-everyone-right) — Talk by Zach Holman on the complexities of time in software
- [Calendar Facts](https://xkcd.com/1930/) — xkcd

## Standards & Specifications

- [IANA Time Zone Database](https://iana.org/time-zones) — The authoritative source for time zone data (also known as tzdata or the Olson database)
- [IETF CALEXT Working Group](https://datatracker.ietf.org/wg/calext/about/) — Active IETF working group for calendaring extensions
- [IETF RFC Editor](https://www.rfc-editor.org/) — Search and browse all published RFCs

## Community

- [CalConnect DEVGUIDE GitHub](https://github.com/CalConnect/DEVGUIDE) — Source for this site; contributions welcome
