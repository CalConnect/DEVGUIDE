Known CardDAV client implementations.

## Native

- **Apple Contacts** (macOS, iOS)
- **GNOME Contacts / Evolution** (Linux)
- **KDE Kontact** (Linux)
- **Thunderbird** (cross-platform)
- **DAVx⁵** (Android) — CalDAV/CardDAV sync adapter
