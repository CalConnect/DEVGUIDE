CardDAV hosting providers.

## Major Providers

- **Google Contacts** — CardDAV access for Google accounts.
- **Apple iCloud** — CardDAV sync for Apple Contacts.
- **Fastmail** — CardDAV address book hosting.
- **Nextcloud** — Self-hosted with CardDAV.

## Self-Hosted

- **Radicale** — Lightweight server.
- **Baikal** — PHP-based.
- **SOGo** — Scalable groupware.
