CalDAV hosting services and providers.

## Major Providers

- **Google Calendar** — Free CalDAV access for personal and Google Workspace accounts.
- **Apple iCloud** — CalDAV sync for Apple Calendar users.
- **Nextcloud** — Self-hosted collaboration platform with CalDAV.
- **Fastmail** — Email provider with CalDAV calendar hosting.
- **mailbox.org** — German email/hosting with CalDAV.
- **Rackspace** — Cloud hosting with CalDAV support.

## Self-Hosted Options

- **Radicale** — Lightweight Python server.
- **Baikal** — PHP-based CalDAV/CardDAV server.
- **Nextcloud** — Full collaboration suite.
- **SOGo** — Scalable groupware server.

## Choosing a Provider

Consider: storage limits, shared calendar support, scheduling (iTIP) support, CalDAV scheduling (RFC 6638) support, and mobile/desktop client compatibility.
